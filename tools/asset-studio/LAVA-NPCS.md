# Lava NPCs and monsters (8/1 and 8/2)

Both maps contain one each of Kül Sandalı, Lav Yaran, Cehennem Kalyonu,
Magma Kaplumbağası and Alev Leviathanı (same population rule as tiers 1–7).
Original tier-8 IDs, stats, radii, quest targets and rewards are retained.

Assets: public/assets/lava-{boat,sail,galleon,turtle,leviathan,portraits}-v1.webp.
Ships 8×2 (16 slots from 8 views), monsters 4×2 (N, NE, E, SE, S, SW, W, NW),
portraits five cells.

Source fixes (views assigned by bow/head direction): Kül Sandalı SE = labelled
NW, SW = labelled SE, W and NW mirrored. Lav Yaran SW = "REFERANS GÖRSEL".
Cehennem Kalyonu W mirrored from E, SE mirrored from SW (referans), NE mirrored
from NW. Magma Kaplumbağası SE mirrored from SW. Alev Leviathanı (unlabelled)
SE/SW swapped, NW mirrored from NE.
