# Void NPCs and monsters (7/1 and 7/2)

Both maps contain one each of Karanlık İzci, Obsidyen Bıçağı, Hiçlik Savaşçısı,
Gölge Yengeci and Uçurum Krakeni (same population rule as tiers 1–6).
Original tier-7 IDs, stats, radii, quest targets and rewards are retained.

Assets: public/assets/void-{boat,sail,galleon,crab,kraken,portraits}-v1.webp.
Ships 8×2 (16 slots from 8 views), monsters 4×2 (N, NE, E, SE, S, SW, W, NW),
portraits five cells.

Source fixes: Karanlık İzci SE = mirrored SW. Obsidyen Bıçağı labels were
scrambled; views reassigned by bow direction (E/W swapped, SE/SW swapped,
NE = labelled NW, NW and W mirrored). Hiçlik Savaşçısı sheet had two "E"
cells: the right-facing one is E, the left-facing one W. Gölge Yengeci sheet
had no labels; directions assigned by pose, SE = mirrored SW. Uçurum Krakeni
SW uses the "REFERANS GÖRSEL" cell.
